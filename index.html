FAQ

Questions? We’ve got answers.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Get in touch and we’ll help.

Is HireWise free to use?

Posting a job and receiving offers is free. When you hire, a 5% platform fee is added at checkout — $37.50 on a $750 job — and you’ll always see it before you pay.

When do I pay the professional?

When you accept an offer, the payment is authorised and held securely in escrow. It’s released to the pro only after you confirm the job is complete.

How are professionals verified?

Every pro verifies their identity, phone number and email. Regulated trades such as electricians and plumbers also upload their professional licence, and businesses can add their registration. You’ll see the badges on every offer and profile.

Do I have to pick the cheapest offer?

Not at all. Compare price, rating, completed jobs, availability and each pro’s note — or send a counter-offer before you accept. Most customers choose on overall value.

How long does bidding stay open?

You decide: 2, 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours, or a custom window. Longer windows usually attract more offers, and you can hire a pro at any point.

What if something goes wrong?

Report a problem from the job screen — a no-show, a dispute, a payment issue or anything else. Our support team reviews every report within 24 hours, and your payment stays protected while we sort it out.

Can I offer my own services?

Yes. Choose “Offer services as a professional” when you sign up, or switch on Professional Mode in Settings at any time to start receiving client jobs.
